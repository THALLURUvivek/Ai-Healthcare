04 Buying questions

The things teams ask us most often.

If your question is not here, our team answers same-day, usually within an hour.

Do we have to replace our current care management software?
No. Most teams run Clarity alongside what they already use. It reads the signals that are already there and adds the context your teams are missing, without a rip-and-replace project.
How does Clarity avoid alert fatigue?
Every signal has to earn its place. Clarity ranks by likely impact, collapses duplicates, and quietly retires anything your team consistently dismisses, so the list stays short enough to actually work through.
What happens to our data if we leave?
You can export everything at any time in standard formats, and on exit we delete your data on a schedule you choose, with a written confirmation at the end.
Can we start with just one care pathway?
That is the recommended path. Start with a single pathway such as post-discharge recovery, prove the improvement, then expand once the team trusts the signals.

03 The difference

The old way vs. the Clarity way.

Not a promise about the future. A description of what changes in the first ninety days.

Comparison of traditional care coordination and the Clarity approach
DimensionTraditional coordinationWith Clarity
Starting the dayScrolling charts and inbox threads to find who changed overnight.A ranked list of the patients who need a decision, with the reason why.
Knowing what changedManual chart review, usually after the window to act has passed.Signals appear the moment the pattern does, with the evidence attached.

04 What we commit to

Four promises we will be held to, in writing, for as long as we work together.

Show our work

If a signal cannot be explained in plain language, it does not ship. Every insight traces back to real data.

Keep the final call human

We surface and draft. Your clinicians decide, and their decision is always the one that stands.

Earn the data

No ads, no resale, and no training on your data. Ever. Our business model depends on your trust, not your records.

Measure what matters

We report on outcomes and time returned to care, not on how many alerts we generated.

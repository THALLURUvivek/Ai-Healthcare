Escalation rules

How a case moves between bands

Configure
01
Vitals breach a critical threshold

Any SpO₂ below 90, systolic above 180, or a new neurological deficit moves a case straight to critical.

Automatic
02
Two missed check-ins

Routine cases drift to elevated, elevated to urgent. The patient is notified at the same time.

48 h window
03
No action inside the band window

Urgent cases untouched for 60 minutes escalate to critical and page the on-call clinician.

Pages on call
04
Blocked on capacity

Waiting cases keep their acuity but surface to the bed manager queue after 90 minutes.

04 Before we meet

Fair questions.

If any of these are still open after the call, we will keep working until they are answered.

Who should be on the call?
One clinical leader and one operational or technical leader is ideal. If your security or compliance team wants to join for the second half, we are happy to accommodate that.
Do you need access to our data beforehand?
No. The first call needs nothing but your context. Any technical or data review happens after, under a mutual NDA and a scoped security questionnaire.
How long does implementation actually take?
Most teams are live on a first pathway in two to three weeks. Larger networks with several EHR instances take longer, and we will give you a real range rather than a best case.
What if it turns out we are not a fit?
We will say so on the call and tell you why. Roughly a third of the conversations end with a referral, and we would rather be useful than close a deal that will not work for your team.

06 Questions, answered

Everything you might be wondering.

Still curious? Our team is one message away and always happy to walk through the details.

How long does it take to get Clarity running?
Most teams connect their core systems and see their first live signals within two to three weeks. A typical rollout starts with one care pathway, proves value quickly, and expands from there.
Will Clarity replace the tools we already use?
No. Clarity sits above your existing record systems and pulls signal from them, so the tools your clinicians rely on keep working exactly as they do today.
Who decides what a signal means for a patient?
Your team does. Clarity surfaces patterns and drafts the next step, but clinical judgment always stays with the people who know the patient. Every recommendation is a starting point, never an instruction.
How is patient data protected?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and logged, and Clarity is SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, and HITRUST certified. We never train models on your data or sell it to anyone.
What does implementation look like?
You get a named implementation lead, a data integration review, role-based training for every team you onboard, and a weekly checkpoint until your first measurable improvement lands.
